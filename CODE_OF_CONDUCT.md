# Elide Toolchain code of conduct

Everyone participating in Elide Toolchain's issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, or other project spaces must treat others respectfully. Welcome people regardless of experience, disability, age, nationality, race, religion, gender, gender identity, or sexual orientation. Critique code and ideas constructively; respect privacy and boundaries; accept corrections and take responsibility for mistakes.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory insults, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing someone else's private information without permission are unacceptable. These rules also apply when representing the project publicly.

Report a concern privately to a repository administrator: [sgammon](https://github.com/sgammon), [darvld](https://github.com/darvld), or [TravisCow](https://github.com/TravisCow), using the private contact routes on their profiles or the Elide community's private messaging. Contact a different administrator if the concern involves one of them. Do not post sensitive reports in public issues. Administrators must keep reports confidential except where disclosure is necessary to investigate or protect participants, and must not retaliate against good-faith reporters.

An uninvolved administrator will assess reports, hear relevant perspectives, and explain the decision privately to the reporter and affected participant when possible. Responses may include a request to correct behavior, a warning, removal of content, or temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces, proportionate to the behavior and its impact. A participant may ask another uninvolved administrator to review a decision. Administrators are subject to the same standards and must recuse themselves from decisions about their own conduct.
